# Member app (/home and every signed-in screen)

Mode: Operate. Audience: members and partners, phone-first, short visits several times a week, sometimes late at night. Task: see who replied or needs a reply, join matched groups, post, ask the Grove Guide, message peers and mentors. States: unconfirmed email, empty feed, out of credits, crisis resources, blocked, suspended, deleting.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your own tree is the spine of the product: it grows through onboarding, sits beside every name, anchors Home and celebrates each level. It refuses the community-app default of a coloured sidebar, KPI cards and an endless undifferentiated feed.

OWN-WORLD: taste pastel-consumer layout on the unchanged brand palette and logo: white panels on the #f8f8f8 surface, a #f5f5f5 sidebar with a white active row, one large radius (20px stepping to 10/8/5), the approved tints read in their own inks, primary buttons in the #222 pill, brand orange for the tree and counts, watercolour tree art, Manrope UI with Instrument Serif page titles, geometric tree icons from leaf and circle primitives.

STORY: The member sees what changed for them, replies or acts in one click, and watches their Sunlight grow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Open-plane labelled sidebar (the fg logo and community top, nav on a 34px pitch, Saved/Family/Settings/Help at bottom). Home: page title with the one finding ("2 replies on your post in Endometriosis"), Write a post as the primary action, the feed in two thirds, the right third your tree with Sunlight to next level and People like you.

FORM: pastel-consumer app style, open-plane shell; taste draw seed 657692864.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
