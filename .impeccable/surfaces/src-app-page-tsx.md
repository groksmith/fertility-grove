# Landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a US adult in infertility treatment (and partners), arriving from a clinic, a friend or search, usually on a phone. Action: Create account with an invite link, or Sign in. Proof: product truth only — privacy commitments, the six clinician-reviewed article previews, how matching works. No statistics, testimonials or member counts (user decision).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Follows fertilitygrove.com: the orange logo centred on white, the brand's watercolour orange trees flanking the headline (the growth film plays once), the near-black pill. The orange's circle masks every picture and marks every list. It refuses the health-category default of soft hero photo, three icon cards and a testimonial row.

OWN-WORLD: White ground like the live site; the approved tints (#ffe7de, #f5f5f5, #dff3ff, #ffe8f3, #e0f6eb) as flat folder bands and specimen tiles; the original fg logo, unchanged; Instrument Serif display, Manrope text; near-black #222 pill buttons, photos masked to a leaf; geometric botanical drawings built from vesicas and circles. No gradients, no shadows at rest, no eyebrows.

STORY: The visitor understands this is a private community for infertility and partners, believes it is safe and matched to them, and asks for or uses an invite.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White ground; the orange logo centred top, Sign in link right; h1 "Fertility Grove" in Instrument Serif ~10vw centred; one sentence beneath ("A private community for people going through infertility, and their partners."); from mid-screen a stack of five tinted folder bands, leaf-cut tabs naming Groups, Learn, Grove Guide, Mentors, Partners; the front folder holds the Create account button.

FORM: one-motif direction (the orange's circle), the live site's hero composition; taste draw seed 1576100232.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: "Where are you right now?" — pick a journey stage, see the groups you'd be matched to, a Learn article and your starting tree.
